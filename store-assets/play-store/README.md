## Play Console Listing Copy

Short description:

`FRC match and pit scouting with team analytics, assignments, and sync.`

Full description:

`The Ninja Scouter is a scouting and strategy app built for FRC teams that need fast, organized data during competitions.`

`Scouters can select events, record match data, complete pit scouting forms, and submit reports for their team. When the connection is unreliable at an event, submissions can be saved locally and retried later, helping scouts keep working from the stands, pits, or queue.`

`Team admins can review collected data, compare teams, inspect performance trends, and use scouting insights to support match strategy and alliance selection.`

`Key features:`

`- Match scouting for supported FRC games`
`- Pit scouting by team`
`- Event-based scouting workflows`
`- Assigned game view for scouters`
`- Firebase sync for shared team data`
`- Offline retry queue for failed submissions`
`- Team analytics, score trends, comparisons, and breakdowns`
`- Registration-code based access for team members`
